Why attendance matters

The honest pros and cons.

Pros of attending

  • You follow lectures while they're fresh, so exams need less cramming.
  • Teachers hint at what matters for exams and internals.
  • You stay eligible for LPU exams without chasing permissions.
  • Many programs add bonus marks for high attendance, so it can raise your grade.
  • You build friendships and a study group early.

Cons of falling short

  • Below 75% you can be stopped from sitting the exam, in some programs subject by subject.
  • Missed classes pile up and are hard to recover.
  • LPU gives no casual leave. Missed classes count unless you have a medical certificate or duty leave.
  • Internal marks and scholarships can be affected.

Advantages of good attendance

What you gain by showing up regularly.

  • Exam eligibility: no stress about being stopped from sitting your exams.
  • Bonus marks: students report that LPU gives extra marks in a subject for high attendance. At 90% or more you get 5 extra marks, 85 to 90% gives 4, and 80 to 85% gives 3. Slabs can differ by program, so confirm yours.
  • Better understanding: regular classes mean you grasp concepts as they are taught.
  • Less cramming: a little revision each day beats panic before exams.
  • Faculty support: teachers notice regular students, and that helps when you need guidance, doubts cleared or a recommendation.
  • Better habits: discipline and time management from first year carry into internships and jobs.
  • Stronger network: classmates and seniors become your study group, project partners and friends.
  • Fewer formalities: no extra forms, permissions or last-minute requests to the department.
  • Peace of mind: a safe buffer lets you take a real day off when you are sick or have an emergency.

LPU things to know

  • Check your real attendance on UMS, the university portal introduced at induction. This app is your personal backup.
  • Duty leave is usually given when you take part in an LPU-organised event. Ask your faculty and keep proof.
  • For illness, submit a medical certificate promptly.
  • Rules and bonus-mark slabs differ by program, so confirm yours with your department or on UMS.
